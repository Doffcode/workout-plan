0:05–0:19 Pair 1, alternate, 90 s rest between

Goblet squat
Weeks 1–3: 3 × 8–12
  • Dumbbell held at your chest
  • Same rules as the squat: knees follow toes, whole foot on the floor
  • Go down slowly and pause 1 s at the bottom while learning

Avoid: Heels lifting; Knees caving in

If it hurts or the machine is taken: Leg press

Barbell back squat
Week 4+: 3 × 5–8
  • Bar on your upper back; feet about shoulder width, toes slightly out (whatever lets your knees track over your toes)
  • Big breath into your belly and sides, hold it through the rep
  • Knees go the same direction as your toes; weight across the whole foot (heel, big toe, little toe)
  • Back stays neutral: ribs down, chest not collapsing; go as deep as you can with that position

Avoid: Knees caving in; Heels lifting; Dropping down fast and bouncing

If it hurts or the machine is taken: Leg press or hack squat

Lat pulldown
3 × 8–12
  • Overhand grip about 1.5× shoulder width (try a few; no magic grip)
  • Pull to the upper chest, elbows down and back
  • Let the bar go all the way up for a full stretch every rep
  • Small lean back is fine; no swinging

Avoid: Only doing the top half; Throwing the body back to move the weight

If it hurts or the machine is taken: Assisted pull-up machine

0:19–0:33 Pair 2, alternate, 90 s rest between

Barbell bench press
3 × 5–8
  • Shoulder blades pulled back and down before you unrack; small arch, chest stays high
  • Feet planted, butt on the bench, eyes under the bar
  • Grip a little wider than shoulders (never past ~1.5×); wrists stacked over elbows
  • Lower under control to around nipple line, light touch, press to lockout

Avoid: Bouncing off the chest; Flat back with shoulders rolled forward

If it hurts or the machine is taken: Dumbbell bench press, or machine chest press

Lying leg curl
3 × 10–15
  • Hips pressed into the pad
  • Curl until the pad nearly touches your butt; lower all the way
  • Control the lowering, no swinging

Avoid: Hips lifting; Half reps

If it hurts or the machine is taken: Seated leg curl

0:33–0:47 Pair 3, alternate, 60–90 s rest

Romanian deadlift
3 × 8–10
  • Push your hips BACK, chest comes forward; soft knees that don't travel forward
  • Bar slides down your thighs, close to your legs
  • Back stays flat; stop when your hamstrings are tight, usually around mid-shin
  • The plates should not touch the floor for most people

Avoid: Going to the floor and rounding the lower back; Bending the knees into a squat

If it hurts or the machine is taken: Dumbbell RDL, or 45° back extension

Kneeling cable crunch
3 × 10–15
  • Rope beside your head, hips stay still
  • Curl your ribs toward your hips (round your spine), then stretch back up
  • 10–15 reps, add weight like any lift

Avoid: Sitting back on your heels to pull with your body weight

If it hurts or the machine is taken: Machine crunch or weighted floor crunch

Fast day

Lifting heavy after ~20 hours without food invites dizziness and bad reps, so the squats and deadlifts are on fed days. Today is light weights only, and optional: skip it entirely if you feel weak and nothing is lost. Sip water if your fast allows it. Feel light-headed → stop and sit down. Eat your semiyan and milk soon after the gym.

0:05–0:15 Pair 1, alternate, 60 s rest

Dumbbell lateral raise
3 × 12–20
  • Light weight, 12–20+ reps; this is not a heavy lift
  • Lead with the elbows, slightly in front of your body (15–30°)
  • Up to about shoulder height, a bit higher if it feels fine
  • Slight elbow bend, thumbs level; lower under control

Avoid: Swinging the body; Shrugging the weight up with your traps

If it hurts or the machine is taken: Cable or machine lateral raise

Cable face pull
2 × 15–20
  • Rope at face height, thumbs toward you
  • Pull toward your forehead, elbows high and back, hands end beside your ears
  • Arms straight at the start, brief pause at the top
  • Light weight, 15–20 reps

Avoid: Leaning back to move more weight; Short reps that stop at the chin

If it hurts or the machine is taken: Rear-delt machine fly, or bent-over dumbbell rear raise

0:15–0:25 Pair 2, alternate, 60 s rest

Incline dumbbell curl
2 × 10–15
  • Bench at ~45°, arms hang straight down behind you
  • Curl without moving your elbows forward much; turn palms up as you lift
  • Lower all the way down under control
  • 8–15 reps; this is not a heavy lift

Avoid: Swinging; Stopping before full arm extension

If it hurts or the machine is taken: Standing dumbbell curl or cable curl

Rope triceps pushdown
2 × 10–15
  • Elbows near your sides; full lockout at the bottom
  • Let the rope come all the way back up each rep
  • Elbows can stay still or drift slightly; pick one and keep it the same

Avoid: Half reps with heavy weight; Leaning over the rope

If it hurts or the machine is taken: Overhead cable triceps extension (hits the long head even more)

0:25–0:30 Finish

Calf raise
3 × 10–15, 60 s rest
  • Balls of your feet on the edge, knees straight
  • Pause 1–2 s at the BOTTOM in the full stretch (stops the bounce)
  • Rise up onto the balls of your feet, squeeze, lower over ~2 s

Avoid: Bouncing out of the bottom; Half reps

If it hurts or the machine is taken: Single-leg calf raise on a step holding a dumbbell

Trap-bar deadlift
3 × 5–8
  • Stand in the middle of the bar, handles in line with the middle of your feet
  • Hips back and down until you can grab the handles, chest up, back flat
  • Big breath, push the floor away, stand tall; lower the same way
  • Lower back must not round; if it does, the weight is too heavy

Avoid: Rounded lower back (what the reel showed); Hips shooting up first

If it hurts or the machine is taken: Leg press + an extra set of RDL

Pull-up
3 × 5–10 (assisted if needed)
  • Overhand grip about 1.5× shoulder width; grip the bar hard
  • Start from a full hang; pull shoulders down and elbows toward your back pockets
  • Chin clearly over the bar; a rep that doesn't make it doesn't count
  • Lower under control to a full hang; legs still, no kipping

Avoid: Half reps; Swinging the legs

If it hurts or the machine is taken: Assisted pull-up machine or lat pulldown; reduce assistance over time

0:19–0:33 Pair 2, alternate, 90 s rest between

Incline dumbbell press
3 × 8–12
  • Bench at 30–45°; adjust to where you feel the upper chest
  • Feet flat, butt stays on the seat, chest up
  • Shoulder blades down but not pinched hard back
  • Lower until the dumbbells lightly touch the outside of your shoulders, then press to full lockout

Avoid: Stopping halfway down; Butt lifting off the seat

If it hurts or the machine is taken: Incline machine press

Seated cable row
3 × 10–12
  • Sit tall, slight knee bend
  • Let the handle pull your arms long and shoulders forward for the stretch
  • Pull to your lower ribs, elbows close; chest up
  • Torso moves only a little

Avoid: Rocking back and forth like rowing a boat

If it hurts or the machine is taken: Machine row

How a session works

Pairs
Each block is a pair. Do a set of the first exercise, rest, a set of the second, rest, back to the first. Finish all rounds, then the next pair. Each muscle still gets ~3 min rest, but the clock only runs ~1.5.
Clock
The times on each block (0:05–0:19…) count from when you walk in. Glance at your phone: if you're behind, keep going in order and cut from the bottom.
Late?
Drop the last set of each pair, never the first exercise. The first pair matters most.
Rest
90 s between moves in a pair; 60 s for small exercises. Rest longer only if your breathing hasn't settled.
Crowded?
If the gym is crowded and you'd lose the spot, do the pair as straight sets (all sets of one, then the other) with 2 min rest and drop one set of the second exercise.
Log it
After the last set, write weight × reps of your best set and tick the exercise. Next time, try to beat it.
Taken?
Machine taken? Big lifts: use the listed swap. Small exercises: do the next one and come back.

Adding weight

Weeks 1–2
Learn
2 sets of everything instead of 3. Stop every set with ~3 reps still in the tank. Film your squat, RDL, bench and deadlift once and compare with the GIF.
Week 3 on
Build
Full sets. Stop with 1–2 reps in the tank (0–1 on the last set of small moves). When every set reaches the top of the rep range, add the smallest weight step next time.
When stuck
Reset
Same weight, same reps 3 sessions running → take 10% off that lift and build back up. Two or more lifts stuck at once, or joints achy → one easy week: half the sets, same weights.
